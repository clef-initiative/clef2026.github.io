CLEF 2026 will take place at the Campus Carl-Zeiss-Straße 3 of the **Friedrich-Schiller-Universität Jena** in the heart of Jena, Germany. The conference venue is conveniently located in the city center, making it easily accessible and ensuring accommodation options are within walking distance.
Signs on site will guide you to the rooms where the conference sessions will be held.

More information will follow shortly.

## Wi-Fi

[Eduroam](https://eduroam.org/) is available at the venue (and at many places in Jena). If you are a member of an institution that participates in the Eduroam network, you can use your home institution credentials to access the Wi-Fi, e.g., by using Eduroam's [CAT tool](https://cat.eduroam.org/).

If you cannot use Eduroam, please use the free guest Wi-Fi network below:

- SSID: `guests.uni-jena.de`
- Username: `clef-2026`
